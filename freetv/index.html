Video 05 · Free My TV

A home screen with no ads

Free My TV is free, open source and built with Claude Code. Google TV, LG and Samsung.

1 · Developer mode on your TV

  1. Google TV

    Settings > System > About. Press Android TV OS build 7 times. Then Developer options > USB debugging.

  2. LG

    Install the Developer Mode app from the LG Content Store. Sign in with a free LG developer account. Turn on Dev Mode Status and Key Server.

  3. Samsung

    Open Apps and type 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote. Turn on Developer mode and put your computer's IP in Host PC IP.

2 · Ask Claude Code

  1. 1

    On a computer on the same Wi-Fi, open Claude Code and paste this:

    Install Free My TV (github.com/simbasjungles/free-my-tv) on my TV at [your TV's IP]. I live in [your city].
  2. 2

    Claude builds the app and puts it on your TV. It asks before anything important.

  3. 3

    Your TV's IP: Google TV in Settings > Network & Internet. LG and Samsung in the Developer Mode app or the network settings.

Undo it

  1. Google TV

    Free My TV > Home Screen > Original.

  2. LG

    One command:

    python3 webos/install.py <IP> --uninstall
  3. Samsung

    One command:

    python3 tizen/install.py <IP> --uninstall

Honest catches

  • On LG and Samsung your TV's own home stays. Free My TV is one button away.
  • LG developer mode lasts about 40 days. Press Extend in the Developer Mode app.
  • 2023 and newer Samsungs need your Samsung account once. No computer? TizenBrew Installer, type simbasjungles/free-my-tv.
