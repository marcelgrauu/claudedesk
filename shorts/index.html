Make animated shorts with Claude

Claude Animated Shorts: a free skill that builds 55 to 70 second story shorts in code and renders them on your own PC.

Set it up

  1. 1

    You need Claude Code, Node, Python 3 and ffmpeg.

  2. 2

    Copy the repo into your skills folder, in a folder called cartoon-3d-short.

    git clone https://github.com/mromidev/claude-animated-shorts ~/.claude/skills/cartoon-3d-short
    GitHub ↗
  3. 3

    Have a Remotion project with @remotion/three installed.

    Remotion ↗@remotion/three ↗

Your first short

  1. 1

    Open Claude Code in your Remotion project and give it one true story.

    First prompt
    Use the cartoon short skill and make a short about soldiers who marched across a bridge until it fell down.
  2. 2

    Approve the script. Claude checks every fact and puts the sources on top.

  3. 3

    Add your voice: record it or use a text to speech tool. The skill times everything to your words.

  4. 4

    Let your PC render it. The author's 67 second short took about 10 minutes.

Good to know

  • Renders copy a lot of files to your temp folder. Set REMOTION_TMP to a big drive first.
  • Story, characters and voice should be your own.
  • It's a community skill (MIT), not made by Anthropic.
