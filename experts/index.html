Video 27 · Make Claude decide

Put 3 experts in one chat

Tired of "it depends"? Make Claude argue with itself, vote, and give you a real answer.

The prompt

  1. 1

    Open a new chat, paste this and put your question on the first line:

    3 experts
    Help me decide: [your question]
Pick the 3 experts who would disagree most about this. Give each one a role.
Round 1: each expert argues their side in 2 lines.
Round 2: they challenge each other once.
Round 3: they vote.
Then give me a clear verdict, no "it depends", and the one thing I should check first.
  2. 2

    Read the fight, not just the verdict. The best argument is often the losing one.

  3. 3

    Do the one thing it tells you to check first. Then decide.

Good to know

  • Works for anything: a job offer, moving cities, a big purchase.
  • Add your facts (budget, deadline, what you care about) and the experts get sharper.
  • It's a second opinion, not a professional one. For money, health or legal calls, check with a real expert.
